I use seismic methods to unveil the structure and properties of the Earth’s crust and mantle.

I am a Brazilian geophysicist, and my path in science has taken me through Norway, Italy and Germany. I work with seismic waves from ambient noise and earthquakes, using tomography, attenuation, anisotropy and receiver functions to learn what lies beneath our feet. What I enjoy most is data analysis: turning large and noisy seismic datasets into robust images and models that other researchers can use and test.

  • Ambient-noise tomography
  • Surface-wave attenuation
  • Radial anisotropy
  • Receiver functions
  • Crustal composition
  • Data analysis
